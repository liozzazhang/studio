one_click_assistant

Bene Click: keypress-driven macro configuration, presets, and sharing for player-defined ability sequences.

Project status: Personal prototype.

Design focus

Player keypresses advance a configured ability sequence, with interaction centered on macro configuration, presets, and sharing. The project does not read ability cooldowns, procs, or resources; in-game behavior and version compatibility were not verified here.
