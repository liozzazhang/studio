agent-harness

A design for a governed execution layer around MCP-driven operations agents, applying policy, approvals, locking, idempotency, and audit to actions with side effects.

Project status: Design & pseudocode.

Design focus

Explores explicit boundaries around agent actions and workflows that can be resumed and traced. Current material is architecture and pseudocode, rather than a runnable execution service.

此页仅介绍项目的功能和设计思路。源码需要单独申请权限。
