设计关注

关注短期凭据从申请、使用到撤销的生命周期，以及命令行、Python 与 Ansible 使用同一核心逻辑时的行为一致性。

vault-approle-toolkit

Obtain short-lived, least-privilege credentials through Vault AppRole, with a shared credential lifecycle across CLI, Python, and Ansible integrations.

Design focus

Focuses on the lifecycle of requesting, using, and revoking short-lived credentials, and consistent behavior across CLI, Python, and Ansible integrations.
